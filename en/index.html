TODAY AT THE NEWSPAPER CAFE

Numbers and words!

Haj Salhafa sits at his cafe table with the paper and a glass of tea. In front of you are crossing equations with blanks. Drag number tiles from the tray and fill them in until every equation is true. Each puzzle has exactly one solution, so no guessing. And beside the numbers sit four Arabic word games: Letter Wheel, Riddle of the Day, Secret Word and Proverb Column.

How to play

Easy to learn from the first puzzle, with a brain-tickling finish.

  1. 1
    7+=12

    Drag a tile

    Every equation has five cells: number, sign, number, equals, result. The missing numbers wait in the tray below. Drag one (or tap it, then tap a blank) and drop it in place.

  2. 2
    9−4=

    Equations cross

    A true equation glows turquoise; a false one turns brick red and shakes. Equations share cells, so one wrong number can spoil two equations at once.

  3. 3
    3×4=12

    Exactly one solution

    Every puzzle is built and re-checked by a solver to have exactly one answer. Logic gets you there, not luck. Stuck? Asfoura flies in and locks the right tile in place, 3 hints free per puzzle.

The cast

Two regulars of the Fawazeer Cafe. They watch every move and react.

Cafe owner and puzzle host

Haj Salhafa

A wise old tortoise in a tarboosh, glasses and a white moustache. He reads the paper at his table with a glass of tea, cheers when you solve, says "oops" when you slip, and thinks it through with you when you are stuck.

The cheeky sparrow

Asfoura

A small sparrow in a blue cap who flies in with hints. She goes to a wrong or empty blank and locks the right tile there. She chirps and teases you whenever you get close to the answer.

7 issues × 20 puzzles

140 hand-checked puzzles, from plain addition and subtraction up to the Golden Issue with multiplication and division. The next issue opens once you solve 12 of the one before.

Daily puzzle

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Your first solve of the day pays coins plus a streak bonus, and each day in a row raises the streak and the reward.

Free play

Random puzzles from any issue you have opened, at the difficulty you pick. No limits, play at your own pace.

Every puzzle is rated 1 to 3 stars from your mistakes, hints and time, and replaying can earn the stars you missed.

Word Fawazeer

Next to the number puzzles there are four Arabic word games, at the same cafe table. Each has levels or a pack, plus a daily puzzle.

Letter Wheel

Swipe across the wheel's letters to spell the words of a crossword grid. A valid word outside the grid is a bonus word worth a coin, and Asfoura has a hint ready.

Riddle of the Day

Haj Salhafa reads an Egyptian riddle and the answer's letters arrive scrambled: put them in order by dragging or tapping. The faster you solve it, the more points.

Secret Word

Guess a five-letter word in six tries. Turquoise means the right place, mustard means in the word but elsewhere. A new word every day and a day streak.

Proverb Column

An Egyptian proverb arrives scrambled: put its words in order in the column, and Asfoura explains what it means.

Coins, stars, the shop, trophies and the free 48-hour pass are shared by numbers and words, and the menu has a strip with today's four puzzles.
